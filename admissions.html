Admission Process

Admissions to F.Y.B.A., F.Y.B.Com. and F.Y.B.Sc. (Hospitality Studies) go through the University of Mumbai's online Samarth admission portal.

  • Register and fill the application form on the Samarth portal.
  • Select Laxmibai Sitaram Halbe College, Dodamarg as your preferred college.
  • Check the merit list on the website and notice board.
  • Visit the college office with original documents and pay the fees to confirm admission.

Deposits

Refundable deposits (library, laboratory, etc.) are charged at admission and returned when the student leaves the college. Contact the office for details.

Scholarships & Insurance

Eligible students can apply for Government of Maharashtra scholarships on the MahaDBT portal and for central schemes on the National Scholarship Portal. The college scholarship section helps with applications.

  • MahaDBT Scholarship Portal ↗
  • National Scholarship Portal ↗

Student Group Insurance

All students are covered under the University's Student Group Insurance scheme. The premium is collected with the admission fees.

Examinations

Semester examinations follow the University of Mumbai calendar. Timetables, hall tickets and results are announced on the notice board and website.

For photocopy and revaluation of answer books, apply through the University of Mumbai portal within the given deadline.
