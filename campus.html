Not Me, But You

National Service Scheme (N.S.S.)

The college has run an NSS unit since 2002. About 150 student volunteers join every year and complete around 120 hours of community service. Activities include village surveys, tree plantation, cleanliness drives, blood donation camps and health and literacy awareness.

Every year a special residential camp is held in an adopted village, where volunteers work with local people on development projects.

Unity & Discipline

National Cadet Corps (N.C.C.)

The NCC unit builds discipline, leadership and patriotism. Cadets take part in parades, training camps and national celebrations, and can earn NCC certificates that help in defence and government careers.

Cultural Activities

Students show their talent at the annual cultural festival, university youth festivals, debates, elocution, music, dance and drama competitions.

Sports

The college encourages fitness and sportsmanship. Students take part in inter-collegiate and university-level tournaments, and the Annual Sports Meet is a highlight of the year.

Department of Lifelong Learning & Extension (DLLE)

DLLE extension projects connect classroom learning with community needs and build skills through fieldwork. Students earn extra credit for completing them.

Research

Faculty members carry out major and minor research projects and publish their work, which builds a research culture on campus.
