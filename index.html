About the College

Higher education for rural Konkan, close to home

Laxmibai Sitaram Halbe College of Arts, Commerce & Science was founded on 6 August 2001 by Navnirman Shikshan Sanstha. Its aim was to bring quality higher education to students of Dodamarg taluka and the nearby Goa–Karnataka border region.

Today the college is affiliated to the University of Mumbai and recognised by the UGC under Sections 2(f) and 12(B). It helps students grow academically and personally, and to serve their community.

  • Affiliated to University of Mumbai
  • UGC 2(f) & 12(B) Recognised
  • NAAC Accredited (2017, 2023)
  • ISO Certified Institution
  • Active NSS & NCC Units
  • Online Library (OPAC)
