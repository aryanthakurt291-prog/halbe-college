Faculty of Commerce

Bachelor of Commerce (B.Com.)

A three-year programme that builds a strong base in accounting, economics, business law and management. It prepares students for banking, finance, taxation and entrepreneurship, and for professional courses such as CA, CS and MBA.

B.Sc. Hospitality Studies

A career-focused three-year degree that combines classroom learning with practical training in hotel and tourism operations. The college's location near Goa offers strong opportunities in the hospitality industry.
