About the College

Laxmibai Sitaram Halbe College of Arts, Commerce & Science, Dodamarg was established on 6 August 2001. It began as a computer-oriented college, created so that students of this hilly, rural taluka would not have to travel far for a degree.

Dodamarg taluka lies at the southern tip of Sindhudurg district, bordering Goa and Karnataka. The college serves students from farming families and remote villages, and many of them are the first in their family to attend college.

The college is affiliated to the University of Mumbai, recognised by the UGC under Sections 2(f) and 12(B), and accredited by NAAC.

Parent Body

Navnirman Shikshan Sanstha

Navnirman Shikshan Sanstha is the trust that manages the college. It works to make quality education available and affordable to every student in the region.

Purpose

Vision & Mission

Vision

To be a leading centre of higher education in rural Konkan that gives students knowledge, values and skills for life.

Mission

  • Provide affordable, high-quality higher education to rural students.
  • Build employability through skill-based and career-oriented programmes.
  • Instil social responsibility through NSS, NCC and extension activities.
  • Encourage research, innovation and lifelong learning.
  • Support all-round development through sports and cultural activities.
